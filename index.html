Rules & How to Play

  • Objective: Win the most duels across 5 rounds using tactical card plays.
  • The Deal: Both players are dealt 5 random cards from a standard 52-card deck at the start of the match.
  • Starting Turn: A coin flip determines who plays first in Round 1.
  • Duels: Players take turns playing 1 card to the battle area. Once both cards are down, the higher card wins 1 point.
  • Card Ranks: High to low: Ace (14) > King (13) > Queen (12) > Jack (11) > 10 through 2. Suits do not affect rank.
  • Ties: Matching card values result in a push—no points are awarded to either player.
  • Tempo Control: The winner of each duel must play first in the following round!
  • Victory: Whoever has the highest score after all 5 cards are played wins the match.
