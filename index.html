Rules of the Game

Overview & Objective

Two players clash in a fast-paced 5-round battle of card ranks. Score the highest points over 5 duels to claim victory.

Game Flow

  1. The Opening Toss: A random 50/50 coin flip decides who plays first in round 1.
  2. Dealing Hands: Both players receive a hand of 5 cards from a standard deck.
  3. Playing Duels: Take turns playing 1 card face up into the battle center.
  4. Scoring Points: High card wins the duel and earns 1 point. Standard rank hierarchy applies: A (High) > K > Q > J > 10 > ... > 2 (Low). Suits are disregarded.
  5. Next Turn Lead: Whoever wins the duel leads the first card in the next duel. If a duel ends in a tie, no points are awarded and the turn order remains unchanged.
  6. Winning the Match: After all 5 rounds are played, the player with the most points wins!
