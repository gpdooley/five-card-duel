How to Play

Five Card Duel is a tactical 5-round card game where players compete for the highest score using single-card matchups.

  • 50/50 Coin Flip: A random flip determines who leads the first card at the start of every match.
  • Card Ranks: Standard rank order applies: Ace (High) > King > Queen > Jack > 10 ... 2 (Low). Suits do not affect card strength.
  • Winning Duels: The higher rank played earns 1 point. In the event of a tie, no points are awarded.
  • Turn Control: The winner of each duel earns the right to lead the next card. In ties, turn order remains unchanged.
  • Winning the Match: The player with the highest total score after 5 rounds wins the game.
