Five Card Duel



Objective

Build the best 5-card poker hand.


Game Play

• Played in Single Player or Two Player mode.

• Each round, 5 cards are dealt face-up.

• Players take turns picking 1 card at a time.

• In rounds 1–3, Player 1 picks first. In rounds 4–5, Player 2/Computer picks first.

• Unchosen cards are discarded at the end of each round.


Poker Hand Rankings

1. Royal Flush | 2. Straight Flush | 3. Four of a Kind | 4. Full House | 5. Flush | 6. Straight | 7. Three of a Kind | 8. Two Pair | 9. One Pair | 10. High Card
